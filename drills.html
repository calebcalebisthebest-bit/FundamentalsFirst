Form Shooting

Shooting

Build shooting consistency by perfecting your form from a close distance.

View Instructions

Duration: 10-15 minutes

Equipment: Basketball, Hoop

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Position yourself 2 feet directly in front of the basket
  2. Set your feet shoulder-width apart with knees slightly bent
  3. Shoot 10 shots using only your dominant hand, focusing on perfect form
  4. After 10 makes, take 2 steps back and repeat
  5. Continue until you're at the free-throw line
  6. Focus on follow-through and consistent release point

Tips for Success:

  • Move to the next distance only after 10 consecutive makes
  • Shoot off the catch with a quick release
  • Keep your elbow tucked and eyes on the back of the rim
  • Record your shooting percentage to track progress

Figure 8 Dribbling

Dribbling

Improve ball handling and court awareness through challenging dribble patterns.

View Instructions

Duration: 10-15 minutes

Equipment: Basketball, Open Space

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart in an open area
  2. Dribble the ball in a figure-8 pattern through and around your legs
  3. Start slowly, focusing on ball control and accuracy
  4. Complete the pattern continuously for 2 minutes without looking down
  5. Gradually increase speed while maintaining control
  6. Switch to your off-hand and repeat

Progression Levels:

  • Beginner: Dribble slowly, 1 minute per hand
  • Intermediate: Increase speed, 2+ minutes total
  • Advanced: Add crossover dribbles, alternate quickly

Wall Passing

Passing

Develop accurate passing technique and wrist strength through repetitive wall passes.

View Instructions

Duration: 10 minutes

Equipment: Basketball, Wall

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Stand 8-10 feet from a flat wall
  2. Mark a target spot on the wall at chest height
  3. Perform 30 chest passes, aiming for the target every time
  4. Catch the return pass cleanly with proper hand positioning
  5. Perform 30 bounce passes to the wall
  6. Finish with 20 one-handed passes with each hand

Focus Points:

  • Step into each pass for full power
  • Use proper thumbs-down follow-through
  • Maintain consistent distance and target accuracy
  • Track how many times you hit the target

Defensive Slide

Defense

Build defensive footwork and lateral agility with controlled sliding movements.

View Instructions

Duration: 10-15 minutes

Equipment: Open Court or Gym

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Assume defensive stance: feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent
  2. Slide laterally across the court without crossing your feet
  3. Keep your hands active and in proper defensive position
  4. Slide the length of the court and back (2 sets)
  5. Add backward sliding: slide forward facing the basket, then backward
  6. Increase speed while maintaining proper form

Key Defensive Principles:

  • Never cross your feet when sliding laterally
  • Keep low center of gravity throughout
  • Stay between your opponent and the basket
  • Use quick, controlled movements, not big steps

Catch and Shoot

Shooting

Develop quick release and game-ready shooting skills with moving passes.

View Instructions

Duration: 15-20 minutes

Equipment: Basketball, Hoop, Rebounder (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Find a partner to pass you the ball
  2. Start at the wing (side of the key area)
  3. Have your partner pass to you
  4. Catch the ball with feet ready to shoot (jump stop)
  5. Shoot immediately with a quick release
  6. Move to different spots around the perimeter
  7. Complete 10 makes from each spot

Shooting Spots:

  • Wing (left and right)
  • Corner (left and right)
  • Top of the key
  • Top right and left

Full Court Dribble

Dribbling

Build endurance and ball security while dribbling at game pace.

View Instructions

Duration: 10-15 minutes

Equipment: Basketball, Full Court

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Start at the baseline with the basketball
  2. Dribble with control to the opposite end at game speed
  3. Perform a crossover dribble at half-court
  4. Continue to the basket and perform a lay-up
  5. Return to the starting baseline
  6. Complete 5 passes down and back per hand

Variations:

  • Speed Variation: Start slow, gradually increase tempo
  • Ball Protection: Use off-arm to shield defensive pressure
  • Eyes Up: Keep focus on court, not the ball
