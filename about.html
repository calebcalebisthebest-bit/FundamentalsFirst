Frequently Asked Questions

What age group is Fundamentals First for?

Fundamentals First is designed for basketball players aged 12–15, though older players can certainly benefit from practicing fundamentals. The drills and explanations are tailored for this age group's development level.

How long are the drills?

Most drills take 10–20 minutes to complete. This length is ideal for developing technique without overwhelming beginner players. You can do one or more drills per training session.

Do I need special equipment?

No. Most drills require only a basketball, a hoop, and open space. Some drills benefit from a wall or partner, but everything is optional and adaptable.

How often should I practice?

For best results, practice at least 3-4 times per week. Consistency matters more than duration. Regular, focused practice beats occasional intense sessions.

How do I track my progress?

Each drill has a completion checkbox. Use my progress tracker to see how many drills you've completed and set goals for improvement.

Can I use this if I play on a team?

Absolutely! Many players use my drills as supplementary training to improve specific skills or work on weaknesses identified by their team coach.
