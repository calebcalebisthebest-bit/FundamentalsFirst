❓ Quick Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about training and the website.

How do I report a technical issue on the website?

Please use the contact form above and select "Report an Issue" as your subject. Provide as much detail as possible about what you experienced.

Can I schedule a private coaching session?

Yes! Contact us about private training availability. I offer sessions for individual players and small groups at my facility and other locations.

Are there team discounts available?

We offer special rates for full teams and school programs. Contact us to discuss partnership opportunities.

How can my school get involved?

Schools can partner with Fundamentals First for after-school programs, team development, and special clinics. Reach out to discuss possibilities.

Do you offer scholarships or financial assistance?

We believe every young athlete deserves access to quality training. Contact us to discuss financial arrangements and scholarship opportunities.

Can I give feedback about the website content?

Absolutely! I'd love to hear your suggestions. Use the feedback option in my contact form to help me improve your experience.
