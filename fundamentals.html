Shooting

Essential Skill

Great shooters aren't born; they are made through perfect practice. Shooting is the most critical skill in basketball. Learn the B.E.E.F technique to become a consistent, reliable scorer.

🎯 B.E.E.F Technique

B
Balance

Position your feet shoulder-width apart with knees slightly bent. Your shooting-side foot should be slightly forward.

E
Eyes

Keep your eyes focused on the back of the rim throughout the entire shot. Don't watch the ball.

E
Elbow

Keep your elbow tucked in, forming a 90-degree angle. Your shooting arm creates a straight line.

F
Follow Through

Snap your wrist downward as if reaching into a high cookie jar. Finish with fingers pointing down.

85% Accuracy Goal
500+ Reps Daily

Dribbling

Control & Speed

Control the ball; don't let the ball control you. Good ball handling allows you to move freely on the court and create separation from defenders. Master these dribbling essentials.

⚡ Dribbling Essentials

👆
Fingertips

Use fingertips, not palms. This gives you better control and faster reaction time.

🪂
Stay Low

Keep knees bent and maintain low center of gravity. Triple-threat position ready.

👀
Eyes Up

Look at the court, not the ball. Develop court awareness and anticipation.

🛡️
Protect Ball

Use off-arm as shield. Keep dribble between you and defender.

10+ Crossovers/Min
95% Control Rate

Passing

Team Play

Passing is the fastest way to move the ball and demonstrates great basketball IQ. A good pass is easy to catch, on target, and sets your teammate up for success.

🎪 Passing Fundamentals

🚶
Step Into It

Step toward your target to add power and accuracy to your pass.

👎
Thumbs Down

Rotate thumbs downward for backspin and straight ball flight.

🎯
Target Chest

Aim for chest area - easier to catch and control.

✋
Follow Through

Complete with smooth follow-through, fingers pointing to target.

90% Completion Rate
2-3 Sec Decision

Defense

Strategy & Positioning

Understanding court positions and defensive principles is crucial for team success. Learn the five positions and how to play solid defense that wins championships.

👥 Court Positions

1
Point Guard

Floor leader, ball handler, play initiator

2
Shooting Guard

Outside shooter, scoring threat

3
Small Forward

Versatile wing, perimeter defense

4
Power Forward

Strong inside player, rebounding

5
Center

Tallest player, interior defense

🛡️ Defensive Stance

🦵 Feet shoulder-width, knees bent
⚡ Low center of gravity for movement
🤚 Active hands, avoid fouling
🎯 Between opponent and basket
🔮 Anticipate and react
85% Defensive Rating
12+ Steals/Game
